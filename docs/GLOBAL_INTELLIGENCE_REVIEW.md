- Binance resumed withdrawals in stages from 1 November and said on 26 November that the suspension had been fully lifted. [1][3]

**Reported claims**

- Binance says it replenished the affected assets at its own expense and that all assets remain fully backed. _(attributed to Binance)_ [1][2]
