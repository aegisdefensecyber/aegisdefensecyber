Privacy notice

Aegis Cyber Solutions LLC · aegisdefensecyber.com · Last updated 28 November 2026

This notice explains what information we handle when you use aegisdefensecyber.com, including AEGIS Global Threat Intelligence and ThreatWatch, and when you subscribe to the ThreatWatch email brief. Questions or requests: info@aegisdefensecyber.com.

Using the website

  • No accounts, cookies or analytics. The site has no sign-in, sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and may keep them in its logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads; see the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Your browser only. Features such as “My watchlist” on the Alerts page and saved CVE selections on the Vulnerability Validation page are stored in your own browser (local storage). They are never sent to us, and you can clear them by clearing your browser’s site data.
  • Vulnerability Validation. Targets, tester names and attestations you type stay in your browser. The site does not scan, connect to or store any system you enter.

The ThreatWatch email brief

  • What we collect. Only the email address you enter in the signup form.
  • Why. To send you the ThreatWatch daily brief you asked for. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by subscribing and confirming.
  • Confirmation. After you sign up, you receive a confirmation email. You are not subscribed until you click the link in it (double opt-in).
  • Our email provider. Subscriptions and emails are managed by Zoho Campaigns (Zoho Corporation), which stores your address and subscription status for us, in the United States, under the Zoho Privacy Policy.
  • Email activity. Zoho Campaigns may record whether an email was delivered or opened and which links were clicked. We use these figures only in aggregate, to see whether the brief is useful.
  • No selling or sharing. We do not sell, rent or share your address with anyone, and we do not use it for anything other than the emails you subscribed to.
  • Unsubscribing. Every email has an unsubscribe link that takes effect immediately. After you unsubscribe, Zoho keeps your address only as an unsubscribed record so you are not emailed again. To have it deleted entirely, email us.
  • How long we keep it. While you are subscribed, and after that only as described above.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it, by emailing info@aegisdefensecyber.com. We will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The site and the email brief are intended for security professionals and are not directed at children under 16.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes affecting subscribers will also be noted in the email brief.
